Fun things

Interests & Hobbies

Sharing some of my favorite activities that make my life more colorful:)

Music

Piano

Piano was one of the first things I ever learned to really focus on, and it's stayed a quiet constant since. I don't perform much these days, but sitting down to work through an old piece, or stumble through a new one, is still my favorite way to reset.

Music

Indian flute (Bansuri)

Learning the bansuri introduced me to a completely different musical tradition, one built more on breath and phrasing than sheet music. It's humbling in the best way, there's always another note or slide to get right.

Staying active

Working out & long walks

Between strength training and long walks with no particular destination, staying active is less about hitting a number and more about clearing my head. Some of my best thinking happens a couple of miles into a walk.

Photography

Photography

I got into photography as a way to actually slow down and notice things: light, color, small everyday moments. I mostly shoot candid shots and whatever scene catches my eye that day.

Traveling

Traveling

Whether it's across the world or a few hours from home, traveling is where I feel most curious. New places always end up teaching me something about food, people, or myself.

Fun events

Making time for fun

From concerts and cultural festivals to spontaneous outings with friends, I try to say yes to experiences that pull me out of routine. Some of my favorite memories live outside of school and work.

Recipes

Trying out new recipes

I love experimenting in the kitchen, from family recipes passed down over the years to random internet finds I can't resist trying. I'm building out a dedicated space to collect them.
