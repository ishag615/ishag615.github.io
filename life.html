Interests & Hobbies

Beyond the screen, I am drawn to things that require patience and presence — playing piano and bansuri, capturing moments through photography, and exploring new places. I stay grounded through daily movement and energized through good company and new experiences.

Music

Piano

I started learning piano at the age of nine and took lessons for almost four years. It was incredibly formative, teaching me the value of discipline and practice. I still play and try to learn new songs every now and then. Learning the piano also helped me understand the foundations of music which allowed me to translate that understanding into other instruments like the harmonium and flute.

Music

Indian flute (Bansuri)

Storytime: I participated in an orchestra contest where I played the harmonium and carrying the heavy instrument absolutely crushed my back. That's when I decided to learn the bansuri, a humble instrument made out of bamboo which is very easy to transport. It required patience and breath control but it has been a very rewarding experience learning it and performing with it.
