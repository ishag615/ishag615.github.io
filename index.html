Why AI and security?

AI and security are intersecting in ways that matter.

As AI systems become more powerful, they also become more critical to protect. The same AI that can detect threats can also be exploited by attackers.

I am interested in understanding how to build AI systems that are secure by design, not after they are built.

How do we ensure the tools we build to solve problems do not create new vulnerabilities?

That is what excites me about this field.

Why GRC?

Security needs structure.

Governance

Responsible systems

Security is not just technical implementation. It is making sure organizations can operate responsibly.

Risk

Better decisions

GRC creates frameworks that help companies understand risk, make secure choices, and prepare before problems grow.

Compliance

Trust and accountability

Good security programs help teams meet obligations while building trust with the people who depend on their systems.
