Mentoring, Learning and Achieving

Initiatives to make an impact

A leader is someone who inspires others to grow, learn and work together to turn a vision into reality.

I have been fortunate to be mentored by some amazing leaders and I strive to pay it forward by mentoring others. A leadership philosophy that guides my approach is to lead by example.

My leadership initiatives have been focused on creating welcoming and inclusive spaces for my peers to thrive. Read more about my involvement below.

Featured

President of the International Student Council

As President of ISC at Kent State, I helped create spaces where international students can feel seen, supported, and connected to campus life.

Kent State University

Building belonging across cultures

In this role, I focused on bringing students together through inclusive programming, cultural celebration, peer connection, and campus collaboration. This role allowed me to meet people from all around the world as Kent State serves home to students from across 100 countries of the world. Being able to create a space where they could share their story was the most rewarding part of the experience.

A very meaningful event that I got to organize was the Global Safari Fest. This event invited more than 500 participants including students, faculty, and community members. The event features a country fair where attendees are given fake passports to allow them to travel the world without leaving campus. The menu featured studnet submitted recipes. Additionally, the event comprised of a cultural showcase and fashion show.

Leading ISC has taught me how to turn big ideas into organized experiences: coordinating people, timelines, logistics, outreach, and details while keeping the purpose of the work centered on community.
